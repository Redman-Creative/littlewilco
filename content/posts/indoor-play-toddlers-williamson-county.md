Cold and rainy days are coming, and a toddler with nowhere to run is a long afternoon. These are the indoor spots in the county I'd use, with the toddler times, ages and prices posted this fall.

For a free option, [library story time](/blog/library-story-times-williamson-county) runs somewhere in the county almost every weekday morning.

## Toddler open gym

### Let It Shine Gymnastics, Franklin

**General George Patton Drive, Cool Springs.** Preschool Open Play is Wednesdays and Fridays, 9:15 to 10:45am, for ages 6 and under. Wednesday is in the Tumble Gym at number 1886, and Friday is in the Main Gym at 1892.

It's $12 a child or $10 cash, and newborns who stay in a carrier are free. No reservation needed, and a parent stays the whole time. Ask at the desk about the punch card.

Open play pauses during school breaks while the gym runs camps, so check their calendar around Thanksgiving and winter break.

### Shining Light Gymnastics, Spring Hill

**4008 Parkfield Loop North.** Open play runs Wednesdays 9 to 10am, and Fridays in one-hour sessions at 9 and 10:30am. The main gym is for ages 6 and under, and the ninja gym is ages 1 to 10.

It's $10 a child per session, and parents stay on the floor. They also have a punch card for regulars.

### Urban Air Cool Springs, Jumperoo

**1735 Galleria Boulevard, Suite 2, Franklin.** Jumperoo is a toddler time on Friday mornings, 10am to noon, for ages 5 and under. It doesn't run on school holidays or in the summer, and Urban Air doesn't post the Jumperoo price, so call ahead.

Outside Jumperoo, the park opens at 4pm on weekdays. A Shorty ticket for kids 40 inches and under is $14.99, and Urban Air socks are required for everyone, $4.49.

## Indoor play spaces

### Little Diggers, Franklin

**1113 Murfreesboro Road, Suite 315.** An indoor sandbox for ages 7 and under, open daily 9am to 5pm. Pricing is by time:

| Time | First child | Sibling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 minutes | $14 | $12 |
| 1 hour | $18 | $14 |
| 2 hours | $23 | $19 |

Two adults per family get in free, and each extra adult is $5. Sign the waiver on their site before you go.

### We Rock the Spectrum, Franklin

**1113 Murfreesboro Road, Suite 203, in Williamson Square.** An indoor play gym with three-hour open play sessions Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm. For weekend hours, check their Facebook or Instagram.

It's $18 a child, $16 for a sibling, or $25 for all day. Everyone goes shoeless, and adults need socks. Fill out the waiver online first.

### The Monkey's Treehouse, Brentwood

**91 Seaboard Lane, Unit 103.** A play cafe for ages 0 to 8. Hours are Monday through Friday 9am to 2pm, Saturday 9am to 3pm, and Sunday 1 to 5pm.

Open play is $19 for kids 2 and up and $15 under 2. Siblings under 1 are free with a paying child. Two adults come in free with each paying child, then it's $5 an adult. On Wednesday through Friday evenings, "Hoppy Hour" starts at 4:30 for $12.50. They ask you to sign the waiver online ahead of time.

### TN Kids Co, Nolensville

**7180 Nolensville Road, Suite 2H.** A three-level soft play space for ages 1 to 10, open Monday through Saturday, 9am to 7pm. Sundays are for private parties.

An all-day pass is $15 for ages 1 to 2 and $20 for ages 3 to 10. Babies under 1 are $8, or free with a paying sibling. Two adults are included, and each extra adult is $5. Socks are required, and they sell them for $2. Fill out the waiver before you play.

## Worth the drive: Adventure Science Center

**800 Fort Negley Boulevard, Nashville.** The Early Explorations area is a screen-free play space for ages 5 and under. Adults are $22, kids 2 to 12 are $18, and under 2 is free.

It's open Monday, Thursday and Friday 9am to 3pm and weekends 9am to 5pm, and closed Tuesday and Wednesday.

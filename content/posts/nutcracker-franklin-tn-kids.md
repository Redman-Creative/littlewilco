The Nutcracker is a lot of sitting for a toddler, but the music is familiar and the costumes are big, and with a three or four year old it can be magic. These are the options near Franklin this season, from a full ballet to a short tea party version.

## The Franklin Nutcracker

**Franklin Special School District Performing Arts Center, 1030 Excellence Way, Franklin.** Put on by the Fellowship School of Creative Arts.

**Saturday December 12 and Sunday December 13, 2026, at 2pm and 6:30pm.** The school hadn't posted ticket prices or a run time yet when I checked. Watch fellowshipcreativearts.org.

It's close to home and has a local cast, which makes it a lower-stakes first ballet than a trip downtown. With a little one, I'd pick a 2pm show.

## Nashville's Nutcracker at TPAC

**Jackson Hall, TPAC, 505 Deaderick Street, Nashville.** Nashville Ballet's production runs **November 27 to December 27, 2026**, with 2pm matinees on most show dates and 7pm shows on December 5, 11, 12, 18, 19, 21, 22 and 23.

The show is about two hours: two 51-minute acts with a 20-minute intermission.

**Every person needs a ticket, regardless of age.** That's TPAC's rule, and TPAC suggests leaving babies and young kids home from shows that aren't aimed at them, so this one fits a preschooler better than a baby. TPAC also lends free sensory bags with noise-canceling headphones, fidgets and weighted lap blankets, which can help a little one through the loud parts.

An aisle seat near the back makes a mid-show walk in the lobby much easier.

## Made for preschoolers

### Nutcracker Tea, Nashville Ballet

**Fisher Center for the Performing Arts, Belmont University, 2020 Belmont Boulevard, Nashville. Sunday November 22, 3 to 5pm.** Nashville Ballet says it's geared to preschool through elementary ages, though everyone is welcome. There are characters from the ballet, a shortened, interactive performance, a professional photo, crafts and treats.

Ticket prices weren't posted when I checked.

### Nutcracker Camp, Fellowship School of Creative Arts

**Saturday November 14, 10:30am to 2:30pm, ages 3 to 8.** A ballet class, a craft, a Sugar Plum tea party, and a performance by Franklin Nutcracker cast members. It's $85.

For a kid who'll be in the audience at the Franklin show in December, it's a fun way to meet the dancers first.

## A few tips

Watch a clip of the Nutcracker at home the week before, so the music is familiar. Pick a matinee. And if the second act is one too many, intermission is a fine place to call it a night.

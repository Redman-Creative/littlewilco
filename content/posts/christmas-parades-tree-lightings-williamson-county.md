A Christmas parade with a toddler is mostly about the fire trucks, the candy and Santa. Here are the 2026 parades and tree lightings around Williamson County, in date order.

## Friday December 4

### Franklin Christmas Tree Lighting

**The Public Square, downtown Franklin.** The city hasn't posted 2026 times yet. Last year festivities started at 6pm, with free photos with Santa from 6, performances at 6:40, and the tree lit around 7:15. There was no shuttle.

Last year Main Street and the streets around the Square closed for it, and the city sent overflow parking to Bicentennial Park and Pinkerton Park.

### Nolensville Tree Lighting

**Nolensville Town Hall, 7218 Nolensville Road. 7pm.** It follows Storytime with Santa and Mrs. Claus at 6pm in the Town Hall board room, which is first come, first served, one parent per family.

## Saturday December 5

### Franklin Christmas Parade

**Downtown Franklin, 1pm.** Put on by the Downtown Franklin Rotary Club. Streets close at 11:45am, and it runs rain or shine. This year the route runs in the reverse direction from past years, and the Rotary Club posts a spectator map on its site.

The Rotary Club's parade rules note that parking is limited and recommend carpooling or drop-off. One o'clock is right at nap time for a lot of toddlers, so a good stroller recline helps.

### Nolensville Holiday Parade

**11am.** The morning option, if a 1pm parade won't work at your house.

### Fairview Christmas Parade

**Saturday December 5, 10am.** This year's theme is "Hard Candy Christmas." It steps off from Westwood Elementary and follows Highway 96 North to Fairview Boulevard. If the weather is bad, the rain date is December 12.

### Morning with Santa, Brentwood

Not a parade, but on the same morning: Santa is at the John P. Holt Brentwood Library, 8109 Concord Road, from 9 to 11am.

## Sunday December 6

### Holiday at the Station, Thompson's Station

**Thompson's Station Town Hall, 1110 Fountain View Boulevard, 3 to 5pm.** The Thompson's Station Community Association's free afternoon has photos with Santa, crafts, games, food, music and a tree lighting.

### College Grove Christmas Parade

The parade's Facebook page says it runs the first Sunday in December at 2pm, which is December 6 this year, with lineup at 1pm at the College Grove Recreation Center, 6600 Depot Street. They hadn't posted a 2026 event when I checked.

## Monday December 7

### Brentwood Christmas Tree Lighting

**John P. Holt Brentwood Library, 8109 Concord Road. 5:30 to 6pm.** There's hot chocolate from the Friends of the Library and a choir sing-along. A reading of The Polar Express starts at 4:45, and the tickets for it are limited, handed out at the Children's Service Desk starting in early November.

## Saturday December 12

### Spring Hill Christmas Parade

**5pm, rain or shine.** It's an evening parade, so bring layers. The city's 2026 map shows the parade heading north on Main Street from Toone Prados Street to Miles Johnson Parkway.

### Dickens of a Christmas

**Main Street, downtown Franklin. Saturday December 12, 10am to 6pm, and Sunday December 13, 11am to 5pm.** There's no admission fee listed. Father and Mother Christmas hand out treats to children, and there's a Victorian village with dancers, characters and story times. Street parking isn't available inside the festival. A $1 shuttle runs each way from The Park at Harlinsdale Farm, and from Franklin High School on Sunday.

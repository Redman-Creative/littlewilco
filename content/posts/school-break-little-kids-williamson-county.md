When school is out, the older siblings are home, the usual weekday routines pause, and a toddler still wakes up at 6:30 ready to go. This is what's open and worth doing with little ones over Thanksgiving week.

## The dates

**Williamson County Schools and Franklin Special School District are both off Monday November 23 through Friday November 27, 2026.**

Looking ahead, Williamson County Schools' winter break is Monday December 21 through Monday January 4, with students back January 5. Franklin Special School District is out December 21 through January 1, January 4 is a no-school admin day, and students return January 5.

## What's closed or different that week

- **John P. Holt Brentwood Library** closes early, at 6pm, on Wednesday November 25, and is closed Thursday and Friday.
- **Spring Hill Public Library** is open Monday through Wednesday and closed Thursday through Saturday, November 26 to 28.
- **Let It Shine Gymnastics** doesn't hold open play during school breaks because the gym runs camps.
- **Urban Air Cool Springs** opens at 4pm on weekdays, and its toddler Jumperoo time doesn't run on school holidays.
- **The Monkey's Treehouse** in Brentwood is closed on Thanksgiving Day.
- **The Nashville Zoo** is closed during the day on Thanksgiving, though Glow Wild tickets are on sale for that night. **Cheekwood** is closed Thanksgiving day and night.

## Before Thanksgiving: Monday to Wednesday

**Baby Discovery, John P. Holt Brentwood Library, 8109 Concord Road.** Monday November 23, 11 to 11:45am, for ages 6 weeks to 24 months.

**Babies & Books, Williamson County Public Library, 1314 Columbia Avenue, Franklin.** Tuesday November 24, at 9:30 and 10:30am, for 0 to 18 months.

**Thanksgiving camp at Let It Shine Gymnastics, Franklin.** Monday November 23 and Tuesday November 24, 8:30am to 3pm. The TOTS group is ages 3 and 4. It's $75 a day, or $70 for members, with $5 off for siblings. Register through the gym's parent portal.

**Thanksgiving Week Camp at LPG Sports Academy, 109 Rand Place, Suite 2, Franklin.** Monday November 23 through Wednesday November 25, with mornings 9:30am to noon, afternoons 1 to 3:30pm, or a full day. It's a multi-sport camp for ages 3 to 9, with soccer, baseball, basketball, flag football, hockey and dodgeball. A morning or afternoon session is $119, and a full day is $189.

**Indoor play.** Little Diggers in Franklin is open daily 9am to 5pm, and TN Kids Co in Nolensville is open Monday through Saturday 9am to 7pm. My [indoor play guide](/blog/indoor-play-toddlers-williamson-county) has prices and ages for those and the others. Holiday weeks can change hours, so a quick look at their pages is worth it.

**Walking Wednesday at FrankTown Festival of Lights, Williamson County Ag Expo Park, 4215 Long Lane, Franklin.** Wednesday November 25, 5 to 6:30pm. The light show opens November 20, and this is the first night it's open to walkers. More in my [Christmas lights guide](/blog/christmas-lights-franklin-tn-toddlers).

## Thanksgiving Day

**Turkey Trot, Meridian Cool Springs, 5000 Meridian Boulevard, Franklin.** GraceWorks' 27th Turkey Trot. The Kids' Turkey Chase is at 9am for ages 9 and under, $15, and parents can run alongside. The 5K is at 8am, $45, and strollers are welcome on the course. There's a 10K at 7am too, and Race Roster adds a processing fee at checkout.

## The weekend after

**Holly Jolly Festival of Lights at Lucky Ladd Farms, 4306 Rocky Glade Road, Eagleville.** Opens Friday November 27 on select dates. Kids under 2 are free, and ages 2 and up are $12 to $16 online. You can meet Santa there nightly from 6 to 8pm.

**Family movies at the Franklin Theatre, 419 Main Street.** The Santa Clause on Saturday November 28 at 1:30pm, and The Muppet Christmas Carol on Sunday November 29 at 10am.

**Nashville's Nutcracker** opens at TPAC on Friday November 27. My [Nutcracker guide](/blog/nutcracker-franklin-tn-kids) covers that and the shorter versions for preschoolers. And if Santa is on the list, my [guide to seeing Santa](/blog/santa-franklin-brentwood-cool-springs) has the free visits.

## Worth the drive

**Adventure Science Center, 800 Fort Negley Boulevard, Nashville.** The Early Explorations area is for ages 5 and under. It's open Monday, Thursday and Friday 9am to 3pm and weekends 9am to 5pm, closed Tuesday and Wednesday. Adults are $22, kids 2 to 12 are $18, and under 2 is free. Thanksgiving Day hours aren't posted, so check before going that day.

**Glow Wild at the Nashville Zoo.** Nightly from November 13, 5 to 9pm, $27 a person, kids under 2 free with a ticket.

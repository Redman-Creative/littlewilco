Some kids run at Santa and some scream at him, and you won't know which kind you have until you try. These are the places to see Santa around Williamson County this season, starting with the ones closest to home.

## Around the county

### FrankTown Festival of Lights, Franklin

**Williamson County Ag Expo Park, 4215 Long Lane.** FrankTown advertises free photos with Santa during its run, November 20 to December 31, but hasn't posted which nights. The Walking Wednesdays, November 25 and December 2, 9 and 16 from 5 to 6:30pm, are open to walkers. My [Christmas lights guide](/blog/christmas-lights-franklin-tn-toddlers) has the details.

### Storytime with Santa, Nolensville

**Nolensville Town Hall, 7218 Nolensville Road.** Friday December 4 at 6pm, in the Town Hall board room, Santa and Mrs. Claus read a story. It's first come, first served, with one parent per family. The town's tree lighting follows at 7pm.

### Franklin Christmas Tree Lighting

**The Public Square, downtown Franklin. Friday December 4.** The city lists Santa as part of the night. The 2026 schedule isn't posted yet, but last year the festivities started at 6pm with free photos with Santa, and the tree was lit around 7:15.

### Morning with Santa, Brentwood

**John P. Holt Brentwood Library, 8109 Concord Road. Saturday December 5, 9 to 11am.** Kids can meet Santa and hand over their wish list. Last year it was free but took a pass, given out about a month ahead, so watch the City of Brentwood's site in early November.

### Franklin Christmas Parade

Santa rides in the Franklin Christmas Parade on Saturday December 5 at 1pm downtown.

### Dickens of a Christmas, Franklin

**Main Street, downtown Franklin. Saturday December 12, 10am to 6pm, and Sunday December 13, 11am to 5pm.** A Victorian Father and Mother Christmas hand out treats to children. A shuttle runs from The Park at Harlinsdale Farm for $1 each way.

## CoolSprings Galleria

**1800 Galleria Boulevard, Franklin.** The Galleria hadn't posted its 2026 Santa schedule when I wrote this. Last year Santa set up on the lower level at Center Court, in front of Dillard's, from early November through Christmas Eve. Visits were free, photo packages were for sale, and you could book a time online through VIP Holiday Photos. Monday was pet photo day.

Booking a time slot is the move with a toddler, since a long line is hard on little ones.

## Worth the drive

**Cookies and Cocoa with Santa, Lucky Ladd Farms, 4306 Rocky Glade Road, Eagleville.** December 11, 12, 13, 18, 19 and 20. Cocoa or milk, cookie decorating, a reindeer food craft, story time and an ornament, plus a visit with Santa and Mrs. Claus. It's $20 for ages 2 and up and $10 under 2, and tickets have to be bought ahead. You take your own photos.

**Glow Wild at the Nashville Zoo.** Santa is at the zoo's light show from November 27 to December 24. Tickets are $27, and kids under 2 are free with a ticket.

**Bass Pro Shops, 323 Opry Mills Drive, Nashville.** Santa's Wonderland runs through most of November and December, with a free photo. Reservations open in early November at basspro.com/santa.

## A few tips

Go early in the season or on a weekday, when lines are shorter. Feed them first. And if your toddler wants nothing to do with Santa this year, a photo of them looking at him from your hip still counts.
